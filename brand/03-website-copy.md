### FAQ

**Q: How much does a project cost?**
A: Every project is scoped individually based on your needs. Typical engagements range from $75,000 to $250,000+ for complete transformations. We provide a detailed proposal with fixed-fee pricing before any work begins.

**Q: How do you accept payment?**
A: Exclusively via corporate or certified check. We require a 50% deposit to begin and the remaining balance upon delivery. This is intentional — it ensures that every engagement is a committed partnership, not a casual transaction.

**Q: What industries do you work with?**
A: Manufacturing, aerospace, defense, industrial engineering, energy, logistics, and B2B technology. We specialize in companies with complex products, long sales cycles, and high-value deals.

**Q: How long does a project take?**
A: A full transformation typically takes 12–20 weeks from kickoff to launch. Timeline depends on scope, content readiness, and client responsiveness.

**Q: Do you work with startups or small businesses?**
A: We focus on mid-to-large scale B2B companies. If you're a smaller company but operate in a complex industrial space, we'd be happy to discuss whether we're the right fit.

**Q: What technology do you use?**
A: We build on React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS with headless CMS integration (Contentful, Sanity, or Strapi). Hosting on enterprise-grade infrastructure with Cloudflare edge caching. We choose technology for reliability and performance, not trends.
