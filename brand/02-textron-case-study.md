# Case Study: Textron Aviation — "From Digital Relic to Industry Benchmark"

## Client Overview

**Company:** Textron Aviation — a leading aerospace and defense manufacturer, operating for over 90 years. Major brands include Cessna, Beechcraft, and Hawker.

**Industry:** Aerospace / Defense / Manufacturing

**Presence Before Engagement:** 14-year-old legacy website with outdated technology, poor mobile experience, and inconsistent brand representation.

**Engagement Period:** Q3 2024 – Q1 2025

---

## The Challenge: "Before"

Textron came to us with a problem shared by many industrial giants: their digital presence no longer reflected their real-world authority. Their website, originally built in 2010, had been patched and bandaged for over a decade but had never been rebuilt. The result was a site that actively *undermined* their market position.

### The "Before" State — A Digital Relic

| Aspect | Before (Legacy Site) |
|---|---|
| **First Impression** | Generic, template-driven layout that looked dated on arrival. No sense of aerospace precision or luxury. |
| **Page Load Speed** | 6.8 seconds (mobile). Over 40% of visitors bounced before the page finished rendering. |
| **Mobile Experience** | Non-responsive. Pinch-and-zoom required on all pages. 68% of mobile users abandoned within 10 seconds. |
| **Navigation** | 47 items in a single dropdown menu. Users described it as "a maze." |
| **Lead Conversion** | A single generic "Contact Us" form buried in the footer. No qualification, no routing, no follow-up automation. |
| **Brand Consistency** | Six different font families in use. Logo usage inconsistent. Photography ranged from 2008 stock images to iPhone photos of factory tours. |
| **Content Architecture** | 340+ pages with no information hierarchy. Product lines buried 4–5 clicks deep. |
| **Trust Signals** | Outdated certifications listed from 2017. No customer testimonials. No case studies. No video. |
| **Security** | HTTP only. No SSL certificate. Chrome flagged the site as "Not Secure." |
| **Analytics** | None. Textron had no visibility into who was visiting, what they cared about, or where they dropped off. |

### Business Impact of the Legacy Site

- **Lead volume:** Declining 12% year-over-year for three consecutive years
- **Sales team frustration:** 73% of inbound leads were unqualified or incomplete
- **Competitive disadvantage:** Both Gulfstream and Bombardier had launched modern sites in 2022–2023
- **Brand perception:** Internal surveys showed 61% of prospects perceived Textron as "behind the times"
- **Missed opportunities:** Estimated $4.7M in potential revenue lost annually due to poor digital experience

## Our Process — How We Got There

### Phase 1: Discovery & Audit (Weeks 1–3)
- Comprehensive technical audit of existing site
- Competitive analysis of Gulfstream, Bombardier, Embraer, Dassault
- Stakeholder interviews with sales, marketing, engineering, and executive teams
- User experience research with 24 fleet operators and procurement managers
- Content inventory and taxonomy mapping

### Phase 2: Strategy & Architecture (Weeks 4–7)
- Information architecture redesign — from 340 pages to 120 pages
- Content strategy: every page has a single purpose and a clear call to action
- Lead generation funnel design: awareness → consideration → decision
- Personalization strategy: different experiences for OEM buyers vs. aftermarket service customers

### Phase 3: Design & Prototyping (Weeks 8–14)
- Design system creation with 140+ reusable components
- Three complete design concepts presented; client chose "Titanium" — dark, metallic, aerospace-inspired
- High-fidelity prototyping in Figma with full interaction design
- Mobile-first approach that scaled up gracefully
- Accessibility compliance targeting WCAG 2.1 AA (achieved AAA on 90% of pages)

### Phase 4: Development & Engineering (Weeks 15–26)
- Built on Next.js with React, Tailwind CSS, and a headless CMS (Contentful)
- Server-side rendering for SEO performance
- Dynamic image optimization via Cloudinary
- Edge caching via Cloudflare
- Automated visual regression testing
- 99.9% Lighthouse scores across all major page templates

### Phase 5: Launch & Optimization (Weeks 27–30)
- Staged rollout with analytics monitoring at each phase
- A/B testing program for critical conversion pages
- SEO migration with zero traffic loss (preserved all organic rankings)
- Training sessions for Textron's content team
- Post-launch optimization sprint based on real user data

### Phase 6: Ongoing Partnership
- Monthly performance reviews
- Quarterly content refreshes
- Continuous A/B testing program
- Priority support with 2-hour response SLA
